## 🇬🇧 What is POMODORS?

📚 **POMODORS** is an interactive study platform designed to help you master presentation slides one by one 🖼️ by allowing you to upload learning materials (PDF, PPTX, ODP, images, and text) 📄, write down what you understood in your own words ✍️, and receive instant, detailed AI feedback powered by Gemini 🤖—including conceptual corrections ⚠️, missing key points 💡, an academic grade from 1.0 to 7.0 📊, and an optimized summary rewrite 🧠 to elevate your learning process! 🎓
